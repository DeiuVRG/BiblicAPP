# Translation profile: the Apostles' Creed (Symbolum Apostolorum)

Profil de traducere: Simbolul Apostolilor. Source language: Latin (`la`). Targets: English and
Romanian, each made from the Latin. The narrative is in English; the Romanian column and the
Romanian instructions are in Romanian.

## The text

- **Issuing body**: none, and no individual author. The Apostles' Creed is the Western baptismal
  creed that grew out of the Old Roman Creed; its received text is attested in the eighth
  century. The old tradition that the Twelve composed it, one article each, is a legend, not a
  fact. The edition carries the Western Church as its issuing body (`author.name`) with `born`
  and `died` empty.
- Tradition: `undivided` (used by Catholics, Anglicans and Protestants; not part of the Orthodox
  liturgy). Public domain.
- The Latin source is the received text (*textus receptus*): "Credo in Deum Patrem omnipotentem,
  Creatorem caeli et terrae. Et in Iesum Christum, Filium eius unicum, Dominum nostrum, qui
  conceptus est de Spiritu Sancto, natus ex Maria Virgine, passus sub Pontio Pilato, crucifixus,
  mortuus, et sepultus, descendit ad inferos; tertia die resurrexit a mortuis; ascendit ad
  caelos; sedet ad dexteram Dei Patris omnipotentis; inde venturus est iudicare vivos et mortuos.
  Credo in Spiritum Sanctum, sanctam Ecclesiam catholicam, sanctorum communionem, remissionem
  peccatorum, carnis resurrectionem, vitam aeternam. Amen." The edition that the library uses
  is chosen by the owner; this profile follows the text above.
- Form: "**I** believe" (*Credo*), the singular of baptismal profession. Structure: three parts
  (the Father, the Son, the Holy Spirit and what follows from him). The older division into
  twelve articles is an editorial layer, not the structure of the library's sections.
- The creed has no scripture references and no Greek.

## Register and voice

Liturgical Latin: short, parallel, without connectors. Keep the asyndeton of the second part
("passus ... crucifixus, mortuus, et sepultus"). Present and perfect tenses as in the Latin.

## Indicații pentru traducerea în română

Registru liturgic, fără arhaisme. Se traduce din latină, nu se copiază textul liturgic românesc
al Simbolului. Se păstrează „Cred” (persoana întâi singular). Fără referințe biblice în acest
text. Ortografia numelui Domnului („Isus”) este o decizie deschisă pentru lucrările fără
tradiție unică; până atunci „Isus”.
