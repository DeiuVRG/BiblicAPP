# Translation profile: the Nicene-Constantinopolitan Creed (381)

Profil de traducere: Simbolul niceo-constantinopolitan (381). Source language: Greek (`grc`).
Targets: English and Romanian, each made from the Greek. The narrative is in English; the
Romanian column and the Romanian instructions are in Romanian.

## The text

- **Issuing body**: the First Council of Constantinople (381), which received the creed of Nicaea
  (325) and completed it, above all the third article; the text is attested in this form in the
  Acts of the Council of Chalcedon (451). The work has **no individual author**: the edition
  carries the issuing body (`author.name`: the Council) with `born` and `died` empty.
- Tradition: `undivided`. Public domain (a text of the year 381).
- The Greek source is the conciliar form, "**We** believe" (Πιστεύομεν). The liturgical use of the
  East and of the West says "I believe"; this edition does not. The translation says "We
  believe" / „Credem”.
- **No Filioque.** The Greek has "who proceeds from the Father" (ἐκ τοῦ Πατρὸς ἐκπορευόμενον) and
  nothing more. The translation must not add "and the Son"; the Latin addition belongs to the
  history of the creed, not to this text.
- Structure: three articles (the Father; the Son; the Holy Spirit, with the Church, baptism, the
  resurrection and the life of the age to come). In the library each article is a section with a
  label, and a paragraph.
- The creed has no scripture references and the jobs carry none (a Greek source has no grammar of
  references).

## Register and voice

Liturgical and solemn, built in parallel clauses. The second article is a long chain of
participles about one subject (κατελθόντα, σαρκωθέντα, ἐνανθρωπήσαντα, σταυρωθέντα, παθόντα,
ταφέντα, ἀναστάντα, ἀνελθόντα, καθεζόμενον, ἐρχόμενον): render it as relative clauses ("who ... came
down, was made flesh, ... was crucified, suffered, was buried, rose again ...") and keep the order
of the events. Short sentences; no ornament. Keep the parallel phrases ("Light from Light, true
God from true God"; "begotten, not made").

## Greek in the translation

The Greek is not repeated in the translation: every paragraph is translated. If a Greek term is
kept (for example in parentheses), it is an exact copy of the source, in NFC and polytonic as it
stands there, never a transliteration (the checks `GREEK_COPY` and `RESIDUE_GRC` look for the
opposite).

## Indicații pentru traducerea în română

Registru liturgic, dar nu arhaizant. Nu se folosește textul liturgic românesc al Crezului ca
model de copiat: se traduce din greacă, cu formulările glosarului. Se păstrează „Credem” (forma
conciliară), fără „și din Fiul”. Referințe biblice nu există în acest text.
