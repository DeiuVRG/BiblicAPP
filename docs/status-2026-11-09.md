## How to resume

1. New session on this repository, then `scripts/pnpm-install.sh`.
2. The workflow scripts used by the orchestrator are not in the repository (they lived in the
   session scratchpad). Rebuild the remaining steps from `docs/roadmap-v2.md` section 3.5 (P1a
   steps 7–14) and the translation flow in `content/translation/README.md`: for each job run the
   draft, critic and revise prompts of `content/translation/prompts/<lang>/` with Sonnet agents,
   `translation-check --fix` between passes, then `translation-merge`, `build-pack`, add the packs to
   `apps/mobile/src/content/bundled-packs.ts` and the catalogue sources, `build-catalogue`.
3. Signing key: `content/keys/dev.key` exists only in the session container (gitignored). A new
   container needs a new key (`keygen`), its `.pub` pinned in the app next to `dev.pub`, and the
   packs rebuilt; the owner should keep a copy of the private key outside the container.
